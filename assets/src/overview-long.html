What it is

Not a chatbot — a computer that does the work

Muse runs on a per-user Muse Secure VM: an isolated Linux box with a full browser, reachable from the app, WhatsApp or the web. You give goals; it returns when something changed or when it needs your approval.

Muse Secure VM

One isolated machine per user, with a full browser. Other users' agents cannot reach it. You can take over the same machine at any time.

Agent + Sentinel

Muse Spark 1.3 plans and browses. A second, system-level isolated agent (Sentinel) gates every outbound action before it reaches the internet.

15+ connectors

Slack, Stripe, Shopify, QuickBooks, Notion, Zoom and more — plus Facebook, Instagram and Meta ads. Payments run on Link's one-time-use card.

The part most coverage skips

What it records — and where the human stops it

What it records (TIME, 2026-10-06)

  • An hourly dossier on you and everyone you mention — how you met, shared interests, disputes, "tensions and alliances".
  • Non-users are in the data. They get mapped through other people's agents.
  • Nightly analysis of the day's conversations; cross-VM "shared lessons" (Meta: de-identified).
  • "Forget" has edges: it does not guarantee the original message disappears.

Where the human stops it

  • Approval gates before sending, paying, or connecting a new app.
  • Per-connection permissions, instant disconnect, audit trail, VM file export.
  • No training on your data (opt-out is the default state, per Meta).
  • Confidential VM (user-held encryption key) is announced — not shipped as of 2026-10-08.
